# HC III inpatient care — clinician panel spec

> Source: multi-agent clinician panel (midwife, ward nurse, district MO, community-realities lens) → synthesis → adversarial HC III reality-check. Convened 2026-06-06. This doc drives the inpatient build; the reality-checked **Phase 1** below is authoritative for what ships first.

## Synthesis summary

HC III inpatient care is dominated by maternity (labour, delivery, postnatal, newborn) plus a small general/paediatric ward, run on intermittent rounds by often a single night nurse with no monitors, frequent stockouts, and referral hours away over bad roads, all under intermittent power and network. The app's current bare, online-gated Admit form is the wrong shape: it blocks the exact 2am no-signal admission it most needs to capture (verified InpatientHomeScreen lines 44-45/125 and InpatientHomeViewModel line 104 gate on isOnline) and produces no list, so admitted patients vanish. HC III needs an offline-first ward spine first (live census plus offline admission record plus append-only rounds observations), then the safety layers hanging off it: a treatment chart treating not-given-stockout as a first-class honest outcome, deterministic on-device danger-sign alerts (maternal alert-line, PPH, pre-eclampsia, IMCI), a phone-sized partograph-lite plus delivery plus linked newborn record, and structured referral plus discharge that close the episode and feed HMIS 105 for diocese subsidies. Everything is Room-first with one outbox row per intent and immutable device-local timestamps, reusing the deterministic CriticalAlertRules/VisitCriticalAlertBanner pattern and the established admit/dispense RPC outbox path. The district MO cross-facility dashboard is the only piece that lives on web, not the phone.

## Reality-check verdict

Buildable and unusually well-grounded — the spec already avoids the obvious hospital-grade traps (no CTG, no continuous monitors, partograph-lite not A3, stockout-as-first-class, Room-first one-outbox-row-per-intent) and its load-bearing code claims check out: the admit button does gate on isOnline (InpatientHomeScreen.kt:125), the offline banner is at 44-45, admit() re-gates at 104, and CriticalAlertRules.evaluate() is a deterministic single-rule pure function over PatientVitals that the alert work can cleanly extend. The phasing is sane: fixing admit-then-vanish + offline admission + rounds obs as Phase 1 is exactly right and high-value. The real weaknesses are not over-reach on devices/labs (the author disciplined that well) but four safety blind spots the synthesis missed because it reasoned about screens, not the 2am ward: (1) every alert is passive and only fires once the nurse is already looking at the deteriorating patient — there's no escalation to the patient she HASN'T visited; (2) the drug round, where lone-nurse error actually happens, has no weight anchor or dose-sanity check despite an under-5 ward; (3) neonatal resuscitation's golden minute has no acute flow, only an after-the-fact checkbox; (4) on-entry typo validation is missing, so immutable garbage values can fire false PPH/pre-eclampsia alerts or mask real ones. Cut SpO2-as-routine and the alert thresholds that depend on it, keep the newborn nested under the mother (not a separate census admission) until discharge, drop progress-note dictation, and treat app-authored emergency drug DOSING as a separately clinically-signed-off deliverable rather than something the engineering spec ships unreviewed. With those fixes the Phase 1 spine is the right thing to ship first.

## Phase 1 — the ward spine (reality-checked, ship first)

- Replace InpatientHomeScreen (admit-only) with a live ward census home reading from Room: LazyColumn of admission cards showing name, age/sex, ward+bed, admit date + day-of-stay, and time-since-last-obs. Tap opens chart; Admit demotes to a + FAB; simple General/Maternity filter. (Drop sickest-first acuity sort, status dots, and dose/referral chips from v1 — those features don't exist yet to feed them.)
- Remove the isOnline gate (InpatientHomeScreen 44-45/125, InpatientHomeViewModel 104): admission becomes a Room-first optimistic write with a local UUID that opens the chart immediately and queues rpc_admit_patient via the existing SyncEngine outbox/dispense path. This is the core bug fix — the 2am no-signal labour admission must succeed.
- Required admission-type selector (General / Maternity) on admit, branching the chart and feeding HMIS 105 denominators. Capture admission WEIGHT here (load-bearing for paediatric dosing safety later). For Maternity, capture only the minimum that does NOT block the write: GxPx, EDD/gestation, in-labour vs postnatal, HIV status from ANC card — everything else deferred to the chart, never gating Admit.
- Append-only rounds observations chart: numeric-keypad-first sheet (temp, pulse, resp, BP, AVPU) + under-5 IMCI danger-sign checklist (not feeding/drinking, vomiting everything, convulsions, lethargy/unconscious). One immutable Room row + one outbox row per round (never per-keystroke). Device-local timestamp, back-dateable for blackout rounds. SpO2 optional/collapsed, never required, never alert-gating.
- On-ENTRY value sanity validation reused/generalized from the existing infant_high_fever confirm pattern: range-check every vital and prompt 'entered correctly?' on out-of-range values BEFORE saving, so torchlight typos don't store garbage or fire false alerts.
- Deterministic on-device danger-sign ALERT (red VisitCriticalAlertBanner) extended from CriticalAlertRules for the values Phase 1 already captures: temp >=39, resp >30, AVPU P/U, IMCI severe signs → persistent 'consider referral / call the Clinical Officer' banner. Ship the ALERT now; defer app-authored MgSO4/oxytocin DOSING checklists to a separately diocese-clinical-lead-reviewed item. (Pull the maternal PPH/pre-eclampsia thresholds forward into this same engine as a fast-follow once those obs fields land.)
- Obs-overdue ESCALATION, not just a passive badge: an on-device due-timer that raises a real device notification when a patient hasn't been observed within the expected window — because the single night nurse's killer is the patient she hasn't opened, and an in-chart-only banner never reaches her.

## Safety blind spots the synthesis missed (reality-check)

- NEONATAL RESUSCITATION IS TIME-CRITICAL AND THE SPEC HAS NO IMMEDIATE-NEWBORN FLOW. 'Baby not breathing at birth' needs action in the first 60 seconds (dry, stimulate, bag-and-mask) — there is no time to open a separate newborn chart and tap toggles. The delivery record captures 'resus_done' as a checkbox AFTER the fact, but there is no at-birth 'baby not breathing / not crying' immediate alert + Helping Babies Breathe golden-minute prompt. This is the single biggest newborn killer at HC III and the spec misses the acute moment entirely.
- WEIGHT-BASED PAEDIATRIC DOSING SAFETY. The general/paediatric ward is mostly under-5s where every drug and fluid is mg/kg and ml/kg. The treatment chart captures 'dose' as free text with no weight anchor and no flag for a dose wildly out of range for the child's weight. A lone night nurse re-dosing a convulsing child by torchlight is the classic 10x error. At minimum: capture admission weight, surface it on the treatment chart, and add a deterministic 'dose looks high/low for weight' sanity check. The spec's alert engine watches vitals but never watches the drug round, which is where lone-nurse error actually happens.
- MATERNAL/NEONATAL HYPOGLYCAEMIA AND SICK-NEWBORN 'SMALL BABY' DANGER. Hb/glucose is one of the few labs HC III HAS, yet glucose appears nowhere as a danger sign. A floppy/convulsing newborn or a sick child is hypoglycaemic until proven otherwise; LBW (<2.5kg) and preterm babies need warmth + feeding watch. The newborn danger-sign set (not feeding, convulsions, fast breathing, jaundice, hypothermia) is missing low blood sugar and explicit LBW/preterm flagging that should drive a 'keep warm, feed, watch, refer' bundle.
- THE ALARM NEVER REACHES THE SLEEPING NURSE. The whole design assumes a single night nurse covering general + maternity, but every alert is a passive in-app red banner that only fires when she has already opened that patient's chart and entered a value. There is no obs-overdue ESCALATION (the patient she hasn't visited in 6 hours is the one who dies), and no device-level nudge (notification/sound) for a due maternal obs or an unacknowledged critical alert. An alert system that requires the nurse to already be looking at the deteriorating patient solves the wrong half of the problem.
- MAGNITUDE/UNIT SANITY ON OBSERVATIONS ENTRY. Keypad-first one-handed entry by torchlight is exactly where a fat-finger BP of 1600/110 or temp of 390 gets stored — and then silently triggers (or fails to trigger) an alert. The append-only/immutable model means a typo round can't be corrected, only superseded, and a garbage value can fire a false PPH/pre-eclampsia alert (alert fatigue) or mask a real one. Needs on-entry range validation + a confirm-this-value prompt (the existing infant_high_fever rule already does this for temp — generalize it to all vitals at ENTRY, not just at alert time).
- ABO/Rh + REFER-OUT FOR BLEEDING/ANAEMIA IS UNDERSPECIFIED FOR 'NO BLOOD' REALITY. Blood supply is erratic/absent; the highest-stakes maternal decision is recognizing a bleeding or severely anaemic mother EARLY enough to refer while she can still survive the hours-long road. Blood group is captured at admission but never used; there's no 'estimated blood loss + shock signs → this is a REFER NOW, blood not available here' bundle. PPH blood-loss estimation by eye is notoriously unreliable, so the trigger must lean on pulse/BP/consciousness trend + visible-loss, not a single ml number.

## Cuts (do NOT build as specced)

- SpO2 as a routine observation field — HC III pulse oximeters are scarce, often broken, batteries dead, and a single shared probe rarely reaches the ward at 2am. Listing SpO2 inline in the obs sheet trains a nurse to leave it blank every round and makes the row feel incomplete. Keep SpO2 as an OPTIONAL, collapsed field that never blocks a round, and never let an alert rule depend on it (the 'SpO2 under 90' alert thresholds must be cut — they will almost never have a reading, and absence of a number must not suppress the clinical danger sign).
- Newborn modeled as its own full admissions row (ward=newborn) with its own census card and independent local-id reconciliation. This is hospital data-modeling imposed on a one-nurse ward: it doubles the census, creates an orphan-baby reconciliation bug surface on sync, and a baby with no NIN/patient record fights the patient-centered schema. Phase 1-3 should keep the newborn as a child record nested UNDER the mother's admission (one card, expandable), not a separate census entity. Promote to its own patient only at discharge/registration when there's time and signal.
- Dictation on progress notes (even 'best-effort/queued'). Whisper needs network; a queued audio blob that may transcribe hours later, or never, is a clinical-record liability on a ward where the note IS the handover. A 6am handover note that's still an un-transcribed audio file is worse than useless. Typed-only for inpatient progress notes; drop the dictation path entirely from this feature.
- Caregiver/next-of-kin as a structured multi-field capture (attendant name, relationship, NOK phone, logistics note) — over-modeled for could-tier. Collapse to a single optional 'attendant + phone' free-text line on the chart header. The relationship/payment-constraint taxonomy is documentation theater no night nurse will fill.

## Downgrades

- 'Live ward census as inpatient role home' is correctly MUST, but the spec bundles too much into the v1 card (status dot sorted sickest-first, day-of-stay, time-since-last-obs, obs-overdue chip, doses-due chip, referral chip). 'Sickest-first' sorting requires a triage/acuity signal the app doesn't capture yet and will sort wrong, hiding a deteriorating patient mid-list. Phase 1 card = name, ward/bed, day-of-stay, time-since-last-obs only. Defer status-dot acuity sort and the doses-due/referral chips until the treatment and referral features actually exist (Phase 2).
- 'Deterministic on-device danger-sign alerts' is rightly MUST, but the bundled first-response checklists WITH MgSO4/oxytocin/misoprostol DOSING printed in-app is a higher-stakes, higher-liability deliverable than the alert itself. Downgrade the embedded drug-dosing checklists to a separately-reviewed sub-item (must be signed off by the diocese clinical lead against the Uganda Clinical Guidelines before shipping), and ship the deterministic ALERT (the red banner + 'consider referral / call clinical officer') first without app-authored dosing. A wrong bundled MgSO4 dose is a patient-killer the app would own.
- 'Partograph-lite dilatation-vs-time plot with alert/action-line markers' — keep the labour OBSERVATIONS table as MUST, but downgrade the plotted alert/action-line graph to SHOULD. The append-only timed FHR/dilatation/contractions table delivers most of the safety value and the alert thresholds; a correct, legible action-line plot on a phone screen is a real design risk that can ship one phase later without losing the core capture.
- 'Treatment chart with not-given-stockout' stays MUST for the not-given/given record, but the 'optional ward stock decrement reusing pharmacy_stock_movements/rpc_record_dispense' should be downgraded out of the inpatient build entirely for now — coupling ward administration to pharmacy stock sync invites exactly the blocking/duplication bugs the spec elsewhere warns against. Record given/not-given honestly; let pharmacy reconcile stock separately.

## Full feature set (synthesis, MoSCoW)

### [MUST] Live ward census as the inpatient role home

- **Why:** Most-requested across all four voices and the spine every other feature hangs off. Admitted patients vanish today; a lone night nurse covering general ward plus maternity has no roster to round against or hand over. Sort sickest-first with a status dot, show bed/age/day-of-stay and time-since-last-obs, surface obs-overdue/doses-due/referral chips.
- **App surface:** Replace InpatientHomeScreen (admit-only) with a LazyColumn ward board of admission cards (name, age/sex, ward+bed chip, admit date plus day-of-stay, time-since-last-obs, status dot, badge chips); tap opens the chart; Admit demotes to a + FAB; General vs Maternity filter, maternity lists linked newborns. Reads from Room.
- **Data model:** admissions(id uuid local, patient_id, clinic_id, ward enum general/maternity/newborn, bed text, admit_type, provisional_dx, admitting_staff_id, admitted_at device-ts, status enum active/discharged/referred, client_op_id); census queries status=active joined to latest observations per patient.
- **Raised by:** ward-nurse, district-mo, community-realities, midwife

### [MUST] Offline admission record removing the online-only gate

- **Why:** Verified InpatientHomeScreen lines 44-45/125 and InpatientHomeViewModel line 104 disable admit offline; backwards for offline-first since the commonest admission is a woman in active labour at night with no signal. Admission must be a local write opening the chart immediately and syncing later. Required admission-type selector branches the chart and feeds HMIS 105 denominators and the MO appropriate-admission audit.
- **App surface:** Drop isOnline checks on the Admit button (125), banner (44-45), and admit() (104); write optimistically to Room with a local UUID and queue rpc_admit_patient via SyncEngine (dispense pattern); maternity captures GxPx, LMP/EDD, gestation weeks, ANC attendance, HIV status from ANC card, blood group, presenting status (in labour/referred-in/postnatal); bed stays free text.
- **Data model:** Extends admissions with admit_type plus maternity_admission(admission_id, gravida, para, lmp, edd, gestation_weeks, anc_attended, hiv_status, blood_group, presenting_status); client_op_id idempotency; admitted_at from device clock; reuses Room->outbox->SECURITY DEFINER RPC path.
- **Raised by:** midwife, ward-nurse, district-mo, community-realities

### [MUST] Append-only rounds observations chart with trend and obs-due nudge

- **Why:** Care is intermittent rounds by hand, not monitors; the danger is a missed round. The trend (temp climbing, BP falling over three rounds) tells a lone nurse to wake the Clinical Officer or refer. Sub-minute, keypad-first, one-handed by torchlight, with back-dating for rounds written on paper in a blackout. AVPU consciousness and an under-5 IMCI danger-sign checklist are the HC III deterioration signals.
- **App surface:** Observations tab: numeric-keypad-first sheet (temp, pulse, resp, BP, SpO2 if available, AVPU) plus IMCI under-5 checklist (not feeding/drinking, vomiting everything, convulsions, lethargy); trend strip of the last 4-6 rounds reusing KhVitalChip/KhVitalCard, abnormal flagged red; census shows obs-overdue badge from an on-device due-timer.
- **Data model:** Reuse patient_vitals with visit_id nullable plus admission_id FK so it lands on the patient timeline; each round one immutable Room row plus one outbox row (never per-keystroke); device-local timestamp editable for back-dating, preserved on sync (last-write-wins per row); due-time computed on-device.
- **Raised by:** ward-nurse, district-mo, community-realities, midwife

### [MUST] Treatment chart with first-class not-given-stockout outcome

- **Why:** The drug round is the most error-prone lone-nurse night task: doses missed or double-given across handover, or the drug out of stock. A chart that pretends stock is infinite is dangerous and ignored. Separating ordered from actually-given (and why skipped) protects the patient, informs the next decision, and per the MO not-given-stockout is a reportable cross-facility supply signal. Pharmacy owns ordering; this is the ward record.
- **App surface:** Treatment tab: active orders (drug, dose, route, frequency) each showing next-due, a due-now section pinned to top, a due-count badge on the census card; tapping a dose records Given (time plus by-whom) or Not given with a reason chip (stockout/refused/NBM/patient absent/other); drug names from rpc_get_clinic_catalog.
- **Data model:** medication_orders(admission_id, drug_ref, dose, route, frequency, ordered_by, active) plus medication_administrations(order_id, scheduled_time, status enum given/not_given, not_given_reason, given_by, administered_at, client_op_id) append-only, one outbox row per dose; optional ward decrement reuses pharmacy_stock_movements/rpc_record_dispense without blocking on pharmacy sync.
- **Raised by:** ward-nurse, district-mo, community-realities

### [MUST] Deterministic on-device danger-sign alerts plus first-response checklists

- **Why:** They fire when signal is most likely absent, so must run on-device with in-app guidance and zero network; CriticalAlertRules.evaluate() is already deterministic (single infant_high_fever rule) and pairs with VisitCriticalAlertBanner. PPH and pre-eclampsia/eclampsia are the biggest maternal killers; general thresholds (resp over 30, temp over 39, SpO2 under 90, AVPU U/P, IMCI signs) drive a consider-referral nudge. Trigger from values already entered, visual and persistent, never silenceable-only.
- **App surface:** Extend CriticalAlertRules with maternal thresholds (FHR under 110 or over 160, dilatation crossing alert/action line, BP at or above 160/110, PPH blood-loss threshold, symptom toggles headache/visual changes/epigastric pain) and general/IMCI severe-vitals rules; red VisitCriticalAlertBanner with a tap-through first-response checklist (PPH: uterine massage, oxytocin/misoprostol, IV fluids, call for help/start referral; eclampsia: MgSO4 loading dose); banner surfaces a consider-referral prompt.
- **Data model:** No new persistence beyond reading latest observations/labour/postnatal/delivery rows; rules pure functions over PatientVitals/maternity obs on-device; checklist text and MgSO4/oxytocin dosing bundled in the APK as static resources; acknowledgement reuses existing critical-alert response DTOs to record on the timeline on sync.
- **Raised by:** midwife, district-mo, ward-nurse

### [MUST] Structured referral pack with honest outcomes

- **Why:** Referral hours away over bad roads is the highest-stakes HC III decision and most distorted by community reality; families cannot afford transport and patients leave against advice or abscond. If the only outcomes are discharged and referred, the record lies and the Clinical Officer cannot see who slipped through. A structured referral is both a safety handover the receiving offline facility can use and an honest HMIS 105 number; the MO audits referral timeliness facility-by-facility.
- **App surface:** Refer action producing a structured referral record plus a printable/shareable handover summary (admission, latest vitals/partograph state, treatment given, diagnosis, reason) via the existing receipt-printer path; maternity template pulls obstetric summary plus last labour/postnatal obs plus meds given (MgSO4, oxytocin); transport status field; outcome picker feeds discharge close-out.
- **Data model:** referrals(admission_id, reason, decided_at, departed_at nullable, destination, treatment_given_summary, vitals_snapshot, transport_status); marks admission.status=referred; compiled and printed fully offline from local Room; syncs as a timeline event.
- **Raised by:** district-mo, community-realities, midwife

### [MUST] Discharge / outcome capture closing the admission and feeding HMIS 105

- **Why:** An admission with no recorded outcome leaves the census wrong, the bed falsely occupied, and a hole in the diocese HMIS 105 returns (admissions, discharges, deaths, maternal/newborn outcomes) that unlock subsidies. Capture final diagnosis, outcome (recovered/referred/left against advice/absconded/died), length-of-stay, advice; for maternity also resolves the linked newborn and maternal/newborn outcome tallies.
- **App surface:** Discharge action: final diagnosis, outcome picker, short structured advice (danger signs to return for, breastfeeding, family planning, immunisation due for maternity), then drops patient and linked newborn off the active census locally and stamps length-of-stay; rolls up to the existing web admin/reports HMIS pages.
- **Data model:** discharges(admission_id, final_dx, outcome, advice, discharged_at, length_of_stay) closing admissions.status; maternity cascades to the newborn record; offline Room-first outbox write frees the bed in local census immediately; reconciles to HMIS aggregates on sync.
- **Raised by:** district-mo, midwife, ward-nurse, community-realities

### [MUST] Maternity labour chart (partograph-lite) plus delivery plus linked newborn

- **Why:** Maternity is the bulk of real HC III inpatient load and highest-stakes for referral; HMIS 105 maternal/newborn figures originate here and must be captured at the bedside. Labour monitoring is the highest-value maternity safety tool (replacing the paper partograph); keep it phone-sized partograph-lite, not an A3 grid or CTG input, or the midwife reverts to paper. The newborn must become its own monitorable patient.
- **App surface:** Maternity admission opens a Labour tab: time-ordered append-only obs (FHR, cervical dilatation cm, contractions per 10min, maternal pulse/BP/temp, liquor, moulding) with large tap targets, plus a dilatation-vs-time plot with alert/action-line markers feeding the alert banner; Record-delivery form (mode SVD/assisted/referred, time of birth, outcome live/still, APGAR 1 and 5 min, birth weight, sex, resuscitation, third stage oxytocin/blood loss/placenta complete, vitamin K, cord care, breastfeeding initiated); on save creates a linked newborn chart.
- **Data model:** labour_observations(maternity_admission_id, fields, observed_at) append-only/immutable; deliveries(maternity_admission_id, mode, born_at, outcome, apgar_1, apgar_5, birth_weight, sex, resus_done, oxytocin_given, blood_loss_ml, placenta_complete, vit_k, breastfeeding_initiated); newborn is an admissions row ward=newborn linked by mother admission_id via local id until reconciled on sync; feeds HMIS 105 delivery/live-birth/stillbirth/LBW counts.
- **Raised by:** midwife, ward-nurse, district-mo, community-realities

### [SHOULD] Postnatal observations for mother and newborn (first-24h) with danger-sign toggles

- **Why:** Most maternal/newborn deaths happen in the first 24 hours postpartum, yet care is intermittent rounds. Timed postnatal obs for mother (BP, pulse, temp, lochia/bleeding, uterus/fundal height, perineum) and newborn (breathing, warmth, feeding, cord, danger signs: not feeding, convulsions, fast breathing, jaundice, hypothermia) wired into the alert banner. Should because it builds on the must-level observations, maternity, and alert primitives.
- **App surface:** Postnatal tab on both mother and newborn charts: recurring obs entry with last-entry summary pinned at top and a next-check-due hint; newborn danger-sign toggles feed the alert banner.
- **Data model:** postnatal_observations(admission_id, subject enum mother/newborn, fields, danger_sign_flags, observed_at) append-only; reuses observations append plus due-timer plus alert plumbing; device-local timestamps preserved on sync.
- **Raised by:** midwife, community-realities

### [SHOULD] Progress notes timeline plus ward-level handover view

- **Why:** With one person per shift, handover to the morning Clinical Officer who was not there is where patients fall through the cracks. A short timestamped progress entry beats a heavy SOAP note at 6am and is the narrative spine tying obs, meds, and the labour record together. The MO wants a no-review-today flag to audit daily review. On-screen handover is the source of truth; printing optional so a dead printer never blocks shift change.
- **App surface:** Progress tab: short dated entries (typed always-available; dictation reuses the dictate path best-effort/queued since Whisper needs network) on the timeline with author cadre plus timestamp; ward-level Handover view auto-assembles every active inpatient with bed, status dot, last obs, due meds, active alerts, latest note, optional print/PDF; subtle no-review-today indicator on the census row.
- **Data model:** progress_notes(admission_id, body, author_staff_id, created_at) append-only, one outbox row per note via cached staffId; handover renders entirely from local Room; never overwrite a newer remote note blindly.
- **Raised by:** ward-nurse, district-mo, community-realities, midwife

### [COULD] Fluid balance for the sickest patients, opt-in per patient

- **Why:** For the dehydrated under-5 on ORS/IV, the PPH mother, or anyone on a drip, a running in/out tally guides fluid decisions and referral timing where there are no pumps. Lower-frequency than vitals and only for the sickest, so off by default to avoid cluttering the lone nurse screen; correctly ranked could by both voices that raised it.
- **App surface:** Optional Fluids tab enabled per patient: quick-add IN (IV/ORS/oral) and OUT (urine/vomit/blood-loss estimate) via keypad, running shift/24h total and balance at top, small balance figure near the trend area; hidden until turned on.
- **Data model:** fluid_entries(admission_id, direction enum in/out, type, volume_ml, recorded_at) append-only single outbox rows; balance summed on-device; no dependency beyond the phone.
- **Raised by:** ward-nurse, district-mo

### [COULD] Caregiver/attendant plus next-of-kin capture on the admission

- **Why:** At HC III the family attendant does much of the basic care and provides the transport and money for any referral; consent and follow-up run through them. Recording attendant name/relationship, next-of-kin phone, and transport/payment constraints makes referral logistics and follow-up possible. Strictly optional; an unconscious patient may arrive with no known attendant, so it must never block admission.
- **App surface:** Optional fields in the admission form/chart header: attendant name plus relationship, next-of-kin phone, free-text transport/payment-constraint note, shown at the top of the chart.
- **Data model:** Columns on the admissions row (attendant_name, attendant_relationship, next_of_kin_phone, logistics_note), pure local text, all nullable.
- **Raised by:** community-realities

### [COULD] Low-literacy/local-language labels, icons, and tappable danger-sign checklists

- **Why:** Enrolled nurses and family attendants vary in English literacy and patients speak the local language; dense English prose gets misread or skipped where misreading is dangerous. Icon-led status, short imperative labels, and WHO danger-sign checklists as tappable items beat paragraphs. A build-time resource concern; start with danger-sign checklists since they carry the most risk.
- **App surface:** Cross-cutting: icon-led status across inpatient screens, short imperative labels, danger-sign checklists as tappable items, key prompts in the local language where feasible.
- **Data model:** No schema impact; static strings/icons in the APK via Android string resources; applies to census, observations, alert checklists, and postnatal/newborn danger-sign toggles.
- **Raised by:** community-realities

### [SHOULD] Cross-facility inpatient quality dashboard (web, not Android)

- **Why:** The MO thinks in populations and accountability: once bedside capture syncs, a roll-up across HC IIIs (admission and delivery volumes, length-of-stay, referral rate/timeliness, inpatient/maternal/newborn deaths, not-given-stockout signals) exposes inappropriate-admission patterns, under-referring facilities, and supply gaps. Reuses the existing web admin/reports plus HMIS 105 surfaces rather than burdening the phone; downstream of every must above.
- **App surface:** apps/web admin/reports: inpatient/maternity tiles per facility derived from synced admission, observation, delivery, referral, and discharge events, aligned to HMIS 105 (migrations 013/014); read-only, consumed online at district level.
- **Data model:** No new field capture; aggregate views over synced admissions, observations, deliveries, medication_administrations (stockout reason), referrals, and discharges.
- **Raised by:** district-mo

## Maternity-specific

- Maternity admission type capturing obstetric history (GxPx, LMP/EDD, gestation weeks, ANC attendance, parity risk flags, HIV status from ANC card, blood group) and presenting status (in labour/referred-in/postnatal), written fully offline.
- Partograph-lite labour chart: append-only timed obs (FHR, cervical dilatation, contractions per 10min, maternal pulse/BP/temp, liquor, moulding) with a phone-sized dilatation-vs-time plot and alert/action-line markers, not a full A3 grid or CTG input.
- Deterministic on-device maternal danger-sign alerts: FHR under 110 or over 160, dilatation crossing the action line, BP at or above 160/110 with pre-eclampsia symptom toggles, and PPH blood-loss threshold, each surfacing a bundled first-response checklist (uterine massage, oxytocin/misoprostol, IV fluids, MgSO4 loading dose, start referral).
- Delivery/birth record: mode (SVD/assisted/referred), time of birth, outcome (live/still), APGAR 1 and 5 min, birth weight, sex, resuscitation, third-stage steps (oxytocin given, blood loss estimate, placenta complete), vitamin K, cord care, early breastfeeding, feeding HMIS 105 delivery/live-birth/stillbirth/LBW tallies.
